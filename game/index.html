BUTTONS

  • AMove between the icons. In the game, guess LEFT
  • BPick the highlighted icon. In the game, guess RIGHT
  • CGo back. With nothing highlighted, it shows their age

Keyboard: A, B, C or ← → Esc

ON THE SCREEN

  • HUNGER The hearts next to the fork. A meal fills one.
  • HAPPY The hearts next to the star. Snacks, games and events fill them.
  • HEARTS Red is full, an outline is empty. Four is the most.
  • SICK A blinking skull means it's time for medicine.
  • ALERT A blinking ! means they're starving, bored or sick.

TAKING CARE

  • FEED A meal fills a hunger heart. Their favorite snack fills a happy heart. Too many can make them sick, and way too many in a row ends the game!
  • GAME Guess which way they'll jump. Win 3 of 5 rounds for a happy heart.
  • MEDICINE Cures them when a skull shows up. Sometimes it takes two doses.

GROWING UP

Every egg hatches as a BABY, becomes a KID after 1 year and an ADULT after 3. Once they're a kid, surprise EVENTS pop up (a press conference, a state dinner, a surprise vote...). Answer with A or B, or press C to ignore.

A blinking ! means they need you. Keep them going for 8 years (about 15 minutes) to win 2 TERMS. Leave them hungry or sick too long and they get VOTED OUT.
